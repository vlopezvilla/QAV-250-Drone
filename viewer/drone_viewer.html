To use with your ESP32:
1. Flash mpu6050_stream.cpp — it streams roll,pitch,yaw at 115200 baud.
2. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor — only one program can hold the port.
3. Click Connect ESP32 and pick your board's port.
Needs Chrome or Edge for the Web Serial API. Drag the canvas to orbit the camera; the sliders work without hardware.
